[H2: What is NAD+?]
NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every living cell. It's essential for:

Energy production: NAD+ is required for mitochondria to convert food into ATP, the energy currency of cells
DNA repair: NAD+ activates enzymes (PARPs) that fix damaged DNA
Cellular signaling: NAD+ regulates sirtuins, the "longevity genes" that control aging
Over 500 enzymatic reactions throughout the body
Without NAD+, you would die within seconds. With declining NAD+, you age faster.

[H2: Why NAD+ Declines with Age]
NAD+ levels naturally decrease as we age:

By age 40, NAD+ levels have dropped by approximately 50% from their peak
By age 60, levels may be 80% lower than in your 20s
Lifestyle factors accelerate decline: poor sleep, alcohol, stress, overeating
This decline is associated with many hallmarks of aging:

Decreased energy and stamina
Cognitive decline and brain fog
Slower recovery from exercise or illness
Visible skin aging
Increased inflammation
